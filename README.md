#  ResearchHub – University Research Project Management System

> A centralized web-based platform for managing, monitoring, and showcasing university research projects.

##  Project Overview

**ResearchHub** is a web-based **University Research Project Management System** designed to manage and monitor university research projects from **proposal submission to project completion**.

The platform connects **students, supervisors, administrators, and industry professionals** through a centralized system. It supports research project registration, supervisor allocation, document submission, feedback, progress tracking, evaluations, and research analytics.

ResearchHub also provides a **public research project showcase**, allowing ongoing and completed research projects to be viewed by the public and industry professionals. This helps reduce research duplication, increase the visibility of student research, and create opportunities for **internships and industry collaborations**.

---

##  Objectives

- Centralize university research project management.
- Improve communication between students and supervisors.
- Simplify supervisor allocation and project monitoring.
- Track research progress, milestones, and deadlines.
- Provide secure storage for research documents.
- Reduce duplication of research projects.
- Publicly showcase ongoing and completed research.
- Connect industry professionals with student research and skills.
- Provide research analytics and statistics for university management.

---

##  Main Users

###  Students

- Register research projects.
- Submit proposals and research documents.
- Track project progress and milestones.
- Receive supervisor feedback.

###  Supervisors

- Review research proposals.
- Monitor project progress.
- Provide feedback.
- Evaluate research projects.

###  Administrators

- Manage users and supervisors.
- Manage research projects.
- Handle supervisor allocation.
- Manage deadlines.
- Generate reports and statistics.

###  Industry Professionals / Public Users

- Explore published university research projects.
- Discover student research and skills.
- Identify internship opportunities.
- Find potential industry collaboration opportunities.
